How it works

A letter in
under two minutes

Step one

Choose a mood

Five templates, each with its own emotional register, palette, and typeface. You're not picking a design — you're choosing how it should feel when they open it.

Step two

Write what you couldn't say

One field. Eight hundred characters. A live preview of exactly what they'll see. No drag-and-drop. No overwhelm. Just you and the words.

Step three

Enter their email

The only information we collect — and it never touches our database. It goes straight to the delivery API, then disappears. You remain invisible.

Step four

It finds them

They get an email. They click a link. They land on a beautifully rendered page. They never knew it was coming. That's the part that makes it different.
