# Physical letter prototype

Scope: `prototypes/physical-letter/index.html`, a standalone HTML/CSS visual study. Mode: Experience.
User explicitly requested an HTML prototype as the visual approval artifact before any Three.js/R3F or application integration. This is a code-led, brief-pinned direction; additional comp approvals and global identity replacement would contradict that scope. PRODUCT.md, existing room palettes, names, sample messages, and font families remain authoritative.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Correspondence occupies a table. A paper sheet is retrieved from an envelope that remains present, rather than text appearing across a viewport.

OWN-WORLD: The existing dark house surrounds a matte charcoal writing surface. Photographed fibers and imperfect edges carry material. Blush ribbon, ivory wax, indigo button-and-thread, sage glassine, and butter paper with coral tape distinguish five closures.

STORY: Inspect the collection, choose stationery, open its closure, glimpse the letter, then lay it beside its envelope. Live message text sits within real sheet margins.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Quiet wordmark and existing “Start with a feeling.” copy above five freestanding, differently proportioned photographed envelopes. Selected correspondence occupies a large desk immediately below, with its material description and state controls alongside the actual object.

FORM: User-pinned physical correspondence table; no seed key or alternative identity applies. Four selectable static states use simple CSS transitions. No backend, sending, drag physics, or new product architecture.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Approval boundary: this prototype is the artifact to review. Its scoped DESIGN.md documents the study; root DESIGN.md and the live application are not replaced before visual approval. Desktop/mobile, all five themes, four states, keyboard controls and reduced motion must be checked. Future manipulation is intentionally deferred.

Quality reference: https://www.trevornoah.com/books — inspect freestanding object scale, depth and the breathing room around each object; do not copy typography or page design. Existing screenshots in `.tmp/qa` confirm the old flat room and disappearing-envelope construction being replaced in this study.
