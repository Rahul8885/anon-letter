# Recipient opening

The sender's full preview and /m/:slug share TactileLetter.

1. Release the separate closure: satin ribbon, wax seal, thread, fern/glassine band, or coral tape.
2. Click the envelope to lift its flap. The larger folded insert fills the pocket.
3. Click the exposed letter to draw it out and unfold the paper. The envelope moves aside and fades.

Five generated transparent WebP assets per mood share a registered 3:2 frame: back,
front pocket, outer flap, inner flap, closure. The typed stationeryAssets manifest owns
paths, anchors, hinge, click targets, gesture and complementary closure cut contours.
The first click changes only closure transforms/opacity. No canvas, pixel sampling,
texture cloning, runtime repainting, or paper patches are used. Production assets:
apps/web/public/assets/letter-world/layers/. Generation used built-in ImageGen.

The folded insert occupies 78% of scene width and 72% of height, centered at x=11%,
with its top at y=14% near the y=10% hinge. "For you." remains live text. The fully
opened reading sheet geometry and component callbacks are unchanged. Release, flap and unfolding
last approximately 700ms, 850ms and 1100ms respectively.

Each step waits for click, touch or keyboard activation. Targets are at least 44px.
Keyboard focus follows closure -> envelope -> exposed letter -> reading. Visible text
controls provide the same sequence. Read without ceremony and Escape reveal the complete
letter immediately; Read now interrupts unfolding. Preview replay preserves the draft.
Reduced motion starts fully readable and responds to live preference changes.
Failed stationery images offer Read your letter; missing paper uses the selected stock color.

Paper and preview colors share src/lib/letterPalette.ts: dusty blush, aged parchment,
pale blue-grey with dark ink (the user's choice for Night Thought), sage, and warm yellow.
Each photographed envelope has a complementary desk surface. Composer previews use stock
and ink colors; full-preview toolbars share the recipient desk. A native SVG color matrix
tones the existing paper photograph without changing its fibers, folds, or alpha. The
same treatment applies to the folded insert and reading sheet. Night Thought's pocket
has one horizontal opening; its diagonal seams were removed in an ImageGen asset edit.

Verification covers all five themes at 1440px and 390px, closure-only changes, larger
insert dimensions, rapid clicks, keyboard/touch, skip, replay, draft preservation,
reduced motion, live preferences and failed image loading. See review/interactive-letter/checks.json.
Theme stock/ink contrast, matching preview backgrounds and draft preservation are recorded
in review/interactive-letter/palette-checks.json.
Temporary captures are removed after inspection. Original photos remain referenced by
home/gallery and compact composer; user reference files are retained. Generation prompts
and metadata were removed during repository cleanup.
